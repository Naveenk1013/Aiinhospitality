1. The Big Picture

Two ideas, one goal

  • Digital Twin = a live virtual copy of a real hotel, room or process. We test ideas on the copy, not on real guests.
  • Extended Reality (XR) = the family name for VR, AR and MR. It lets us see and experience digital things as if they were real.
  • Together: the twin holds the data, XR lets people walk inside it and see it.
  • Goal: save money, avoid mistakes, train faster, and sell better.

6. Test Yourself 🎯

✅ 5-Point Revision

  • Digital Twin = live virtual copy that is fed real data.
  • Simulation lets you test "what if" without risking real guests.
  • VR = fully virtual, AR = digital over real, MR = both interact.
  • Apps: sales demos, virtual site inspections, safe staff training.
  • Destination marketing uses XR to make travellers feel the place before they travel.
